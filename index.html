Privacy and Data Protection Notice

All information entered in this Board Generator is processed locally within your web browser. No data is stored, collected, or transmitted to any server or database.

The entered information is available only during your current browser session. Refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache or site data, or closing the browser/tab will permanently remove all entered data.

Please download or save your generated board before leaving this page, as the data cannot be recovered once it is lost.
